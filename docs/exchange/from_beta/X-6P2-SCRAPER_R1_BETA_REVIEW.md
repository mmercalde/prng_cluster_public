### Q2 — Source independence and routine coverage

One vote for the two U sources is a conservative treatment of the reported upstream correlation. Two agreeing votes with no dissent is a defensible policy if two independently maintained routes cover the required keys in practice. Agreement rates and matching error patterns alone cannot establish that N and U, or N and O, represent independent evidence. Routine bookends, lag and effective coverage under O's depth limit and the approved GET/POST configuration remain **UNAVAILABLE**. ### Q3 — Quarantine and backfill

The canonical cut protects an **identified** hole, provided terminal deferral runs on the cut prefix. For example, when evening is accepted and the same date's midday is quarantined, that midday still sorts after the published evening. An absent key never enters the quarantine list. If both sources omit an evening and a later complete day lands, the evening sorts inside the published sequence when it reappears. A correctly defined continuity rule can catch the common omission, but §4 currently lacks a sound anchor and traversal. Build the eligible prefix only through coverage established in draw-number order, and then require a canonical prefix and reapply terminal deferral. A date crossed by a physical hole may need to be held as a unit. Exercise an unseen-by-all key that later appears, and exercise each session as the quarantined key on the same date. A confirmed historical single-session date remains a valid fixture. REV5:225-272, REV4:61-108,320-322.

### Q4 — Draw-number continuity

The draft puts lower draw numbers at midday. Canonical storage puts evening first. Two complete days numbered chronologically as 10, 11, 12, 13 are stored as **11, 10, 13, 12**. Even if new keys are traversed chronologically, the last stored key of day one carries 10, so anchoring there falsely expects 11 as the first new draw. Derive the physical frontier from the verified numbered keys at the published boundary, then check gaps and repeats in physical order before emitting the canonical suffix. Keep REV4's transition halt for an evening on the inherited partial day. Require a verified session mapping at an unlabelled one-row boundary and reconcile numbering in O/X overlap before crossing between sources. O can take priority when its mapping is established. Use `DRAW_NUMBER_UNAVAILABLE` when neither route can establish a number for the anchor or the needed key. A published key absent from O's recent page needs an explicit validated fallback or a hold. REV5:202-208,253-276, REV4:35-72, `docs/DAILY3_CONSUMER_CONTRACT_v1.md:445-451`.
